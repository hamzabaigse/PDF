How to Add Text & Logo Watermarks to PDF Files

Protecting intellectual property, branding draft agreements, stamping confidential disclosures, and preventing unauthorized document distribution requires clean, professional watermarking. FreePDFOnline.world allows you to stamp customized text or image watermarks across every page of your PDF documents with custom typography, opacity, rotation, and positioning—100% locally in your browser.

Protect Intellectual Property & Confidentiality

Watermarks act as a powerful visual deterrent against unauthorized copying and digital leakage. By stamping customer names, transaction IDs, or draft notices across sensitive contract drafts, you establish clear provenance. Because our watermarking tool operates entirely within browser memory, your confidential business proposals never touch external cloud servers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can someone easily remove the watermark?
Our tool embeds the watermark directly into the vector layer of the PDF page, making casual removal difficult without advanced editing software.
Can I control watermark transparency so text remains readable?
Yes! You can adjust the opacity slider from subtle watermark transparency (20%-30%) to bold opaque stamps.
Are my files uploaded to any server during watermarking?
No. All watermarking executes locally in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Complete privacy is guaranteed.
