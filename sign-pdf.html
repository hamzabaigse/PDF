How to Electronically Sign & Annotate PDF Documents Online

Signing official documents, contracts, leases, and application forms used to require printing hard copies, signing manually with a physical pen, scanning, and emailing back. With PDF Master Hub's digital PDF Signer & Editor, you can draw authentic electronic signatures, type text fields, insert today's date, and drag every element to exact document locations before burning them into your PDF file permanently.

How to Place Draggable Signatures & Text:

1
Upload Contract
Open your PDF file and navigate to the target signature page using page controls.
2
Create Tags
Click "Create Signature", "Add Today's Date", or type custom text to generate draggable tags.
3
Drag & Position
Drag the signature or text tag to your desired position on the PDF page.
4
Burn & Save
Click "Save & Download Signed PDF" to output your signed, customized PDF document.
