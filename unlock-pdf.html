Understanding Owner Passwords vs. User Passwords

PDF specifications define two levels of security: User (Open) Passwords, which prevent unauthorized individuals from viewing document contents, and Owner (Permission) Passwords, which restrict actions like high-resolution printing, text copying, and form modification. Our client-side unlock utility decrypts and recreates the PDF binary structure without security dictionaries, giving you complete ownership of your files.
