Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

1. Our Core Commitment to Zero-Server Upload Privacy

At FreePDFOnline.world (https://freepdfonline.world), user privacy is our highest priority. Unlike traditional file conversion websites, FreePDFOnline.world operates with a strict 100% Client-Side Processing Architecture.

When you use any tool on our website (including PDF Merger, Splitter, Signer, Image Converter, Compressor, or Text Extractor), your files are processed entirely in local memory inside your web browser using JavaScript and HTML5 APIs. Your documents, images, text content, and signatures are NEVER transmitted to any external server or cloud storage bucket.

2. Data Collection and Log Files

Because processing occurs on your local device, FreePDFOnline.world does not collect, inspect, cache, or store your uploaded files. Standard web hosting log files may record anonymized network access information such as IP address, browser type, referring pages, and access timestamps for security and server performance telemetry.

3. Cookies & Advertising Policy

FreePDFOnline.world uses third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to serve non-personalized or personalized ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites across the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partner network to serve ads to users based on their visits to FreePDFOnline.world and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ad Settings. Alternatively, users may opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Third-Party Vendors & CDN Services

Our web applications load public static JavaScript libraries (such as Mozilla pdf.js, pdf-lib, and FontAwesome icons) via established content delivery networks (CDNs). These CDNs serve static code assets directly to your browser for fast caching and delivery.

5. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Compliance)

We respect consumer privacy regulations globally, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA). Because all file operations run client-side in your local browser sandbox, we do not hold, sell, share, or monetize your documents or personal identification data. You maintain complete control over your browser data and cache at all times.
