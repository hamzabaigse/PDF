At PDF Master Hub (https://freepdfonline.world), user privacy is our highest priority. Unlike traditional file conversion websites, PDF Master Hub operates with a strict 100% Client-Side Processing Architecture.

When you use any tool on our website (including PDF Merger, Splitter, Signer, Image Converter, Compressor, or Text Extractor), your files are processed entirely in local memory inside your web browser using JavaScript and HTML5 APIs.
